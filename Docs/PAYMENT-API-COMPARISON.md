# Payment API comparison for SEB company invoices

Research date: 2026-10-10. Public primary sources only; no vendor contact, registration, account connection, or payment test. This is feasibility research, not confirmation of production eligibility or a selected implementation.

## Required use case

The assistant reads an invoice and supplies structured instructions; the MCP validates and submits them. Payments come from the company's SEB Sweden account to arbitrary Swedish suppliers, usually Bankgiro/Plusgiro with OCR, in SEK, potentially on a future date. One person approves; confirmed volume is 1–10 company invoices/month. The bank/provider must prevent execution without the user's transaction-specific bank approval even if MCP credentials are compromised. A same-iPhone authorization flow is required. Merchant checkout acceptance and generic Swedish account-data coverage do not establish this use case.

The user clarified that their accountant uses Fortnox. The user makes payments and sends the accountant the attachments afterward; the accountant sometimes initiates Skatteverket payments using existing authority. Preserve that workflow. An accounting-system migration, duplicate supplier-invoice bookkeeping, or assuming the accountant's subscription grants the user API access would be inappropriate. A Fortnox route is relevant only if it can submit actual bank instructions under the existing arrangement.

No complete free production fit was verified. Quotes must include setup, monthly minimum/platform charge, per-payment charge, licence/regulated-service coverage, business-account activation, scheduled-payment modules, and bank fees. Free sandbox access does not imply free live payments.

## Extended search: genuine free access and newly found providers

### YAXI: real free bank access, currently not this bank/payment fit

[YAXI pricing](https://yaxi.tech/) publishes the free real-bank allowance above, with SEPA transfers included in service calls; its [getting-started guide](https://docs.yaxi.tech/getting-started.html) distinguishes production from the integration environment. [Terms](https://yaxi.tech/terms/) describe free access as supplied as-is. This is evidence of genuinely free real-bank functionality, not merely a sandbox. The site emphasizes personal/open-source projects in its FAQ and labels the paid plan for production products, so permanent free commercial own-company use still merits confirmation.

The [Transfer API](https://docs.yaxi.tech/transfer.html) supports arbitrary credit transfers, optional dates and bulk transfers where the bank allows them, but examples use SEPA/IBAN and no Swedish giro/OCR product was found. The separate [Collect Payment API](https://docs.yaxi.tech/collect-payment.html) officially supports EUR/eurozone payments; do not generalize that exact restriction to every Transfer method without evidence. Mandatory signing and same-iPhone BankID have not been established.

Coverage was checked beyond a country-level claim. The public [bank-selector JavaScript](https://yaxi.tech/_astro/BankExplorer.astro_astro_type_script_index_0_lang.DUtrFAHI.js) contains Swedish SEB among fallback banks marked `_comingSoon` when missing from the live results. The site's unauthenticated read-only `POST https://api.yaxi.tech/search` with `{"filters":[{"term":"SEB"}],"ibanDetection":true}` returned only a German Sparkasse, not Swedish SEB. Swedbank/Nordea queries returned empty lists. Thus the shipped public selector treats Swedish SEB as not yet integrated on the research date. This is stronger evidence than merely finding the bank name in website code. **Do not build this company's payment path around it today.**

### Aritma: concrete Swedish business-payment API, quote required

[Swedish payments](https://developer.aritma.com/apis/banking/v4/guides/swedish-payments) documents Bankgiro/Plusgiro and OCR; the [payments guide](https://developer.aritma.com/apis/banking/v4/guides/payments) supports `requestedExecutionDate`. The [approval guide](https://developer.aritma.com/apis/banking/v4/guides/payment-approval) explicitly describes SEB setup. It supports BankID signing baskets before forwarding to the bank, or bank-side approval depending on configuration. Some settings are bank-owned and some involve API consent properties. It therefore does not prove that stolen API credentials cannot change approval settings or select another execution path. The [onboarding guide](https://developer.aritma.com/apis/banking/v4/guides/onboarding) also requires bank integration ordering.

The [Swedish pricing page](https://www.aritma.com/se/pricing) requests quotes based on ERP, bank connections, volume and products; no numeric API price or free live tier is published. Its API targets ERP/fintech/larger-company integrations, without confirming or excluding a one-company internal app. This is a stronger new technical lead than an undifferentiated list of open-banking vendors, but it has no verified price advantage.

### Finshark: actual giro initiation endpoint, remaining product gaps

The first-party [OpenAPI v3](https://cdn.finshark.io/docs/openapi_v3.yaml), linked through its [API reference](https://oas.finshark.io/), defines `POST /v3/payments/se-giro-transfer`: BG/PG destination, OCR, amount/currency and source debtor. The request schema has no execution-date field. [OpenAPI v1](https://cdn.finshark.io/docs/openapi_v1.yaml) contains `se-seb` provider identifiers, but this does not prove current SEB corporate giro support. Its [FAQ](https://docs.finshark.io/faqs) requires production approval and describes bank authentication. No numeric tariff or free live allowance found on its [product site](https://www.finshark.io/). Arbitrary supplier use, bank/product coverage, future dating and the enforceable approval policy remain open. Lower-priority candidate than verified SEB corporate giro adapters.

### Free data access and other false matches

- **Enable Banking:** [current knowledge base](https://enablebanking.com/knowledge-base) explicitly requires the integrator's own PISP/equivalent licence for live PIS. Free restricted access to the company's own account data does not unlock payments.
- **Ponto/Ibanity:** [pricing model](https://myponto.com/en/pricing/customer-paying-model/) allows technically capable customers to integrate their own software without a separate API fee, but charges for linked accounts and payments. No current numeric tariff or SEK giro support was verified. Its [PIS product](https://myponto.com/en/products/payment-initiation-service/) describes SEPA. Not a free exact match.
- **GoCardless/Nordigen:** [API setup](https://docs.gocardless.com/docs/getting-started/set-up) covers customer collections and payouts from a funded payment account. The Swedish [scheme](https://docs.gocardless.com/docs/api-reference/schemes) is Autogiro collection. Neither establishes this SEB supplier-giro workflow; data access and payout terminology are insufficient.
- **Brite:** [payout docs](https://docs.britepayments.com/technical-documentation/integrate-instant-payouts/) cover merchant withdrawals to customer bank accounts, with SE/FI payout reference limitations. No source-SEB supplier-giro/OCR fit verified.
- **Trustly:** its [European product](https://www.trustly.com/) does not establish a free live offer or the required arbitrary supplier-giro/source-account combination. US payout API coverage is not Swedish coverage.

The precise conclusion is **no verified zero-cost service satisfying all requirements**, not that free payment APIs never exist.

### Accounting APIs: preserve the accountant's existing workflow

**Fortnox.** Its [SEB integration](https://support.fortnox.se/produkthjalp/bankkopplingar/kom-igang-med-seb) sends supplier payments to SEB for signing. The [published API](https://api.fortnox.se/apidocs) exposes invoice creation, bookkeeping, and payment approval states, but no supported operation that actually submits the payment to the bank was established. `approvalpayment` is not evidence of bank submission, and recording a supplier payment is not execution. [Integration access](https://checkout.fortnox.se/program/15) is advertised at **189 SEK/month excluding VAT on a twelve-month contract**, plus applicable core-software and bank costs. The accountant's access and tax-payment authority do not prove these licences or an API endpoint are already available. No purchase or bookkeeping changes should follow from the current evidence.

**Dooer.** [Free bookkeeping software](https://www.dooer.com/bokforing), [SEB payment support](https://help.dooer.com/v4/bank) and API access all exist. But the [actual public OpenAPI](https://api.dooer.com/docs/openapi.json), found through its [Sales docs](https://help.dooer.com/v4/sales/), contains only twelve `/public/v1/sales/...` paths. Those record sales invoices, incoming payments, payouts and related reporting data; no supplier-bank-initiation endpoint is present. The [payment UI](https://help.dooer.com/v4/accounts-and-payments/payments/payments/) requires bank authentication, possibly new-recipient signing, and payment signing. Its [new-platform announcement](https://blog.dooer.com/welcome-to-new-dooer/welcome-to-new-dooer/) expresses an intention to make all APIs free and open, without proving a shipped payment API. This is a promising-looking free combination that does not satisfy the verified public-API capability test.

**Spiris/eAccounting.** The [official API schema](https://eaccountingapi.vismaonline.com/openapi/v2.json) has writable `SkipSendToBank` on supplier-invoice creation and bank-integration payment states. The [SEB product workflow](https://www.spiris.se/support/bokforing-fakturering/kassa-bank) can automatically send booked supplier invoices for bank signing. Inference: API-created invoices may enter this queue, but exact live behavior remains untested. It changes accounting records as well as payment instructions. [Published software components](https://www.spiris.se/priser) are Starta 199 + integration 119 SEK/month; adding the published small-business SEB 75 gives **393 SEK/month**, before usage/transaction charges and subject to plan and bank-contract eligibility. Driva gives an illustrative **543 SEK/month** on the same arithmetic. Existing fully equipped customers might have no incremental subscription cost. This user is not known to be such a customer and wants to retain their Fortnox accountant workflow, so it is not recommended here.

**Bokio.** [Private API access](https://www.bokio.se/hjalp/integrationer/bokio-api/automatisera-bokforingen-i-bokio-med-api-sa-gor-du/) is free up to 5,000 calls/month, but documents bookkeeping/customer-invoice capabilities. Its [business payment account](https://www.bokio.se/priser/) is provided by Svea; no existing-SEB payment-initiation endpoint was verified.

**Other app routes.** [SpeedLedger supplier payments](https://support.speedledger.se/hc/sv/articles/30979404792221-Leverant%C3%B6rer) use Open Payments, but no supported public payment-submission API was established. [Pleo invoices](https://www.pleo.io/en/invoices) are paid from a Pleo account/wallet. [Wint](https://www.wint.se/sa-funkar-det) is a wider accounting service (advertised from 625 SEK/month), without a verified standalone supplier-payment API. None establishes a free route preserving the user's current account and accountant workflow.

### Decision after the deeper pass

Retain Open Payments and Aiia for the strongest previously established SEB corporate giro evidence, add Aritma as a substantive new technical candidate, and treat Finshark/ZTL as secondary until coverage is confirmed. No price ranking is justified without written low-volume quotes. YAXI proves free live payment APIs exist but has a current SEB coverage blocker. Dooer/Bokio free APIs fail the specific initiation-capability check. The accountant's Fortnox setup does not establish a usable free API path. Direct GCA works in principle but its published fixed cost is excessive for this volume.

Any eventual adapter must have independent approval enforced outside credentials held by the MCP, with recipient/amount-bound authorization and no accessible alternate unsigned route. Provider-specific same-iPhone behavior and the number of BankID interactions remain acceptance requirements, not verified outcomes. The research used public documentation and public read-only bank-directory queries only; no financial-account access, signup, message or payment was performed.

### Open Payments evidence and free-access ambiguity

The [corporate PIS quickstart](https://docs.openpayments.io/docs/quickstart_pis) includes a Swedish giro example with SEB BIC `ESSESESS`, Bankgiro/Plusgiro destination, OCR/invoice reference and requested execution date. Its [current pricing page](https://www.openpayments.io/pricing) gives tailored quotes based on service, volume, banks and solution setup, without numeric prices or minimums.

The [international-payments FAQ](https://www.openpayments.io/international-payments) says licence use is free, advertises no startup fees or lock-up for starting to build, and says users can start for free. It does not define a free production payment allowance. These claims warrant explicitly asking whether 1–10 live supplier payments/month can be free; neither a free live route nor a required paid minimum was established. See `SEB-PAYMENTS-RESEARCH.md` for security and onboarding details.

The deeper pass also checked the [main company FAQ](https://www.openpayments.io/company): its current cost answer simply asks for the use case and offers a quote, matching the pricing page. Treat the free wording on the international-payments page as unresolved marketing language rather than a usable production offer. Open Payments remains a technical candidate, without a verified cost advantage over Aiia.

### Direct SEB route and a bank-price benchmark

SEB's [integration services](https://sebgroup.com/our-offering/cash-management/integration-services) restrict PSD2 API access to certified third-party providers and describe other corporate integration services. No free self-service API entitlement for this company's private MCP was verified. The [official payment-product matrix](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/seb-oss/openbanking/master/PSD2-release-notes/v8/payment_product_availability.md) lists the corporate Swedish domestic alias product, so the bank-side capability exists.

SEB's [partner accounting-system connection](https://seb.se/foretag/digitala-tjanster/koppla-ditt-affarssystem-till-oss/automatisk-bokforing) advertises **75 SEK/month/account**, transfers payment files to online banking for approval, and requires a supported business/accounting system. The [bank price list](https://seb.se/foretag/tjanster/aktuella-priser) lists Bankgiro payments at **1.85 SEK** and Plusgiro at **3 SEK** in this integration category. Ten Bankgiro payments would therefore be **93.50 SEK/month in these bank components**, excluding accounting-system/provider fees and the underlying bank package. This is a benchmark, not a private MCP API quote or a fee assumed to apply to PSD2 payments. Accounting-system API findings and the user's clarified Fortnox workflow are covered above.

Price qualification from the deeper pass: SEB's broader [2026 cash-management tariff, p.12](https://webapp.sebgroup.com/mb/mblib.nsf/alldocsbyunid/B78E7CE648273B67C1256FB80059E92F/$FILE/CM_Standard_Price_List_Sweden_English.pdf) instead lists ERP integration at **275 SEK/account/month**. Both are official publications. Applicability depends on segment/contract and has not been established for this company; 75 SEK must not be presented as a universal bank/API charge.

#### Direct machine submission: GCA

SEB's [2026 Global Corporate Access product sheet](https://webapp.sebgroup.com/mb/mblib.nsf/alldocsbyunid/639A811E943F7D06C12577CA004E6483/$FILE/GCA_product_sheet.pdf) describes direct SFTP/AS2 file integration with an optional Parking Lot Service: payment files wait for authorized people to review and sign in the bank before processing. This is a genuine own-company automation route separate from PSD2 aggregator access. The [2026 tariff, p.11](https://webapp.sebgroup.com/mb/mblib.nsf/alldocsbyunid/B78E7CE648273B67C1256FB80059E92F/$FILE/CM_Standard_Price_List_Sweden_English.pdf) lists **1,500 SEK/month per GCA/GCA-over-EBICS setup**, with implementation quoted separately and **5,000 SEK upfront toward implementation**. That upfront amount is not the total setup price; other bank/payment charges may apply. Poor economics at 1–10 invoices/month (assessment). Same-iPhone approval and an enforceable always-park configuration remain to be confirmed.

SEB's PSD2 restriction is an access policy. It does not establish that writing internal software for one's own payments legally requires a PISP licence; the separate direct corporate channel is precisely why those questions must be distinguished.

#### Legacy and geographic shortcuts checked

- **Bankgiro Link:** a real [SOAP interface](https://www.bankgirot.se/tjanster/kommunikation/bankgiro-link/) with [SEB communication fees](https://seb.se/foretag/digitala-tjanster/filkommunikation) of 40 SEK/month + 15 SEK/file. However, [Bankgirot's migration FAQ](https://www.bankgirot.se/om-bankgirot/bankgirot-i-korthet/bankgirots-transformationsarbete/transformationsarbetet-faq/) says direct payment-file submission is ending except for Autogiro/e-faktura, and the legacy supplier-payment product is withdrawn after 2026. Unsuitable for a new implementation. Swedish giro/OCR payments themselves continue through the banks.
- **SEB Baltic Gateway:** [own-company API/certificate access](https://developer.baltics.sebgroup.com/bgw/documentation/authentication) exists, but no evidence connects it to the user's Swedish SEB account or giro payments.
- **Direct integration partnership:** [SEB's partner FAQ](https://sebgroup.com/our-offering/cash-management/integration-partnerships/faq) targets established cloud providers with a substantial corporate client base; no self-service owner-only exception found.

### Billecta: conditional alternative, weaker approval fit

Billecta's [API reference](https://docs.billecta.com/reference) documents outgoing payments from the company's own Bankgiro through `POST /v1/payments/outgoingpayments`. The `BankIdReferenceToken` is optional by default; support can configure a requirement for valid BankID tokens. Other supplier-payment paths also exist. This does not yet establish bank-controlled approval, transaction binding, replay prevention or enforcement across all credential-accessible execution paths.

The [API introduction](https://docs.billecta.com/api) says production BankID features require the company's own BankID relying-party agreement/certificate and offers certificate-setup help for **5,000 SEK**. This is optional setup assistance, not the ongoing payment API price. [General terms](https://app.billecta.com/ht/GeneralTermsAndConditions) place prices in the logged-in service or an individual quote. SEB debit setup, supplier OCR/date support and full costs remain unverified. Lower priority than bank PIS for the user's security requirement.

### Mastercard / Aiia evidence

The [official SEB adapter notice](https://status.aiia.eu/incidents/9vqtcyskvh4q), resolved on 2026-10-05, explicitly covers corporate BankGiro/PlusGiro payments: provide a message or `Identifiers.CreditorReference`, and the destination name has a 35-character maximum. The [official incident JSON](https://status.aiia.eu/api/v2/incidents/9vqtcyskvh4q.json) was retrieved with curl when the browser research tool could not access the page. This establishes a concrete SEB corporate giro adapter, but not commercial entitlement or every desired feature.

[Mastercard's official API collections](https://github.com/Mastercard/open-banking-eu-postman-collections) include Aiia Pay, and [the Aiia payout example](https://www.postman.com/aiiaapi/aiia-api-workspace/request/y0mn5r6/create-payment) includes destination details and an optional specific execution date. [Aiia's user terms](https://cdn.aiia.eu/public/aiia-terms-of-use.pdf) describe user confirmation of source, amount and recipient followed by bank authorization. They do not establish an enforceable no-exemption contract for this application. No public price/free live allowance was found in reviewed official material. Treat private-app eligibility as unknown.

The collections explicitly offer Aiia Pay to unlicensed customers, while free developer credentials initially expose only sandbox banks. Mastercard's [Developer Evaluation Agreement, section 3.4](https://www.mastercard.com/europe/en/business/open-finance/developer/developer-evaluation-agreement.html) requires separate rights for use beyond permitted testing and says fees may apply. A free developer account does not establish a free production entitlement. The licence-requiring Aiia Enterprise product should not be confused with the licensed-provider Aiia Pay route.

### ZTL evidence

[Domestic initiation reference](https://docs.ztlpay.io/api-reference/operations/paymentinitiationv2/) supports `swedishgiro`, `BANKGIRO`/`PLUSGIRO`, OCR or text references, currency and due date. [Domestic guide](https://docs.ztlpay.io/guides/domestic-payments/) separates payment creation, approval/signing and final status polling. This is structurally suited to outbound business invoice payments, unlike a merchant-only checkout product.

ZTL's [capability rules](https://docs.ztlpay.io/getting-started/capabilities-and-constraints/) say availability differs by environment, partner, bank and product. The [supported-bank endpoint](https://docs.ztlpay.io/api-reference/operations/getsupportedbanksbycountrycode/) requires authentication and was **not queried**; country-level Sweden support does not confirm SEB. Their [terms, section 10](https://www.ztlpay.io/terms-and-conditions/) specify subscription and transaction fees shown in the payment/ERP system. An API schema alone neither grants production access nor proves approval cannot be bypassed.

The initiation reference explicitly permits unsigned execution depending on bank-account configuration. Require mandatory external approval to be locked for this account before using it. ZTL's [zero-transaction-fee publicity](https://www.ztlpay.io/press-release-money-to-be-made-when-entrepreneurs-shrink-bank-fees/) concerns international payments/FX and is not a published free domestic payment-API plan. The [partner terms](https://www.ztlpay.io/legal/) describe a separate commercial agreement and implementation project, without prices.

### Tink evidence

[Pricing](https://tink.com/pricing/) explicitly places Payment Initiation among Enterprise-only products and directs prospects to personalized pricing. [Payment API](https://docs.tink.com/api-payment) lists SEK and Swedish Bankgiro/Plusgiro identifier types. The [changelog](https://docs.tink.com/changelog) announced SEB Sweden payment initiation on 2021-01-13; this is historical evidence, not a current SEB corporate coverage guarantee. [Getting-started documentation](https://docs.tink.com/resources/one-time-payments/start-payment) distinguishes sandbox test providers from individually verified production apps. Public references demonstrate capability, not a free live service or acceptance of this low-volume app.

### Neonomics evidence and cost example

[Nello Pay pricing](https://www.neonomics.io/nello-pay) publishes the figures above in its default Norwegian currency view. The same page advertises free sandbox testing. Its commercial positioning is merchant payments, so the published tariff must **not** be treated as an offer for arbitrary outbound supplier invoices without confirmation.

The [customer FAQ](https://www.neonomics.io/customers-frequently-asked-questions) distinguishes direct API customers with their own PISP licence from a hosted flow using Neonomics' licence; direct payouts depend on setup/use case and may need further due diligence. [API payment documentation](https://docs.neonomics.io/docs/payments-1) lists Swedish BGNR/PGNR schemes and scheduled domestic payments. [Authorization API](https://docs.neonomics.io/reference/continueauth) supports `BUSINESS` and `CORPORATE`, subject to bank capabilities, and describes authorization when SCA is required. This wording does not establish mandatory SCA for every payment.

For **10 business payments/month**, purely illustrative and assuming this tariff applied: recurring provider cost is `5,600 + 10 × 3 = 5,630 NOK/month`, plus the initial 4,000 NOK and any bank fees. This is not a SEK quote, and no currency conversion is assumed. General comparison formula: `monthly fixed/minimum charges + payment count × provider fee + bank fees`, with setup and optional modules separate. At the confirmed 1–10 invoices/month, the monthly minimum dominates the cost; the illustrative published recurring range would be 5,603–5,630 NOK, if applicable.

## Questions that determine a usable quote

Ask the strongest providers for a single Swedish company's internal own-account app at **1–10 company invoices/month**, explicitly **outbound arbitrary supplier invoices**, not accepting customer payments. Request confirmation of SEB company accounts, SEK Bankgiro/Plusgiro and OCR, scheduling, bank-side per-payment approval without authentication exemptions or reusable authorization, and same-iPhone BankID opening/return flow. Ask whether approval covers recipient, amount, source and reference and how status distinguishes accepted, authorized, executed, rejected and unknown. Obtain low-volume pricing including minimum contract term, platform/licence charges, bank fees and production activation before coding against a paid route.
